Learn to read Thai on **iPhone, iPad and Mac**. Meet Dao and Din in Phrae, then explore more of Thailand as your reading grows.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6819503963)

## Start for free

**Learn Thai Script and the Phrae chapter are free.** Explore consonants and vowels, meet the characters, and work through ten Phrae lessons plus Welcome.

## A new chapter every month

Continue with Chiang Mai, Bangkok and Hua Hin, then discover a new chapter every month. All new premium chapters are included in your subscription.

Choose **USD $2.99 per month** or **USD $24.99 per year**, with the same chapter access on either plan. Local App Store prices may vary. The annual plan is billed upfront; subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through Apple.

## Learn in your language

English, German, French and Spanish are supported for the app interface and explanations. Thai remains the language you read and hear.
