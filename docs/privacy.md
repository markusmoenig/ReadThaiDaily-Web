_Last updated: 8 October 2026._

This page describes Read Thai Daily and this website. It will be updated if future versions add accounts, cloud sync or analytics.

## Learning progress

Read Thai Daily stores learning progress on your device in the app’s own support directory. The app currently has no separate user account or cross-device cloud-sync service.

## Audio and text

The app uses Apple’s system speech synthesis to read Thai text. Voice availability and downloads are managed by the operating system. It does not record your microphone or upload your own spoken reading for assessment.

If you choose to copy a Thai word, the app places that word on your device’s clipboard.

## Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription management use Apple’s StoreKit and App Store services. Apple processes payment information. The app checks verified purchase entitlements to decide which chapters are available; it does not receive your credit-card details.

Apple’s own privacy information applies to its services: [Apple privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## This website

This site does not include analytics, advertising scripts, tracking cookies, newsletter forms or account sign-in. Fonts and images are served with the site. The hosting provider may process standard request logs such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver pages.

Links to Apple and other external sites are subject to those sites’ privacy practices. The website’s static implementation does not itself collect learning progress.
