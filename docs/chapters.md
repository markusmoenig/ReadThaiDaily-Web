The story starts in Phrae and expands into the rest of Thailand. Each chapter contains **ten lessons**, with food and everyday language alongside the places Dao and Din explore. New vocabulary builds on earlier lessons and returns in new situations.

Begin with **40 lessons, plus Welcome**, across four chapters. **A new chapter is added every month**, included in your subscription. Each new destination adds local food, culture and useful everyday Thai.

## Phrae

**Free**, together with Learn Thai Script.

![Dao and Din in Phrae](/img/phrae.jpg)

Welcome introduces the companions. The ten lessons then explore the rice field, home, breakfast, weekdays, the market, the river, a garden, rain, a temple and an evening at home.

## Chiang Mai

**Subscription chapter.**

![Dao and Din in Chiang Mai](/img/chiang-mai.jpg)

Pack for a trip, take a bus, find a guesthouse, meet a friend and explore the old city. Along the way, learn numbers and prices, order khao soi and make plans to meet.

## Bangkok

**Subscription chapter.**

![Dao and Din in Bangkok](/img/bangkok.jpg)

Arrive in the city, use a station and train, ask directions, find a café, order lunch, take a river boat and plan a day out. Familiar words return in new settings.

## Hua Hin

**Subscription chapter.**

![Dao and Din by the sea in Hua Hin](/img/hua-hin.jpg)

Explore the sea, sand and shoreline. Learn seven colors through beach objects, weather and seasons, seafood, swimming vocabulary, beach animals, market fruit, cycling and saying goodbye.
