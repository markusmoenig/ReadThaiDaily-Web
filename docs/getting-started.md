You can start Read Thai Daily without knowing a single Thai letter. Begin with illustrated letters and familiar words, then build towards reading a short Thai story—one lesson at a time.

## Meet Dao and Din

Dao (ดาว) and Din (ดิน) live together in Phrae, in northern Thailand. Their everyday world gives the language a setting: a home, a rice field, breakfast, a market and the places they visit together.

![Dao and Din at home in Phrae](/img/phrae.jpg)

**Welcome** is an introduction. Explore the picture and listen to their names. There is no keyboard exercise to get through before you can begin.

## Choose your starting point

- **Learn Thai Script** introduces consonants and vowels individually, with pictures and sound explanations. Choose a class or a vowel group, then explore at your own pace.
- **Phrae** starts with an illustrated rice field. Learn a few meaningful words, try them in short lines, and build your first readable combinations.

You can move between these sections. The script reference is available alongside lessons whenever you forget a symbol.

## A small daily routine

Explore a scene, listen to a few lines, and try building or reading the words. Revisit familiar material before moving on. There is no need to race through a chapter or memorise every symbol in one sitting.

Each chapter has ten lessons and a longer final reading that reuses its vocabulary. Welcome sits before the ten Phrae lessons.

## Choose your teaching language

The app supports **English, German, French and Spanish** for its interface and explanations. Thai remains the language you read and hear. The website and learning guide are also available in these four languages.

## Your devices

Read Thai Daily is a Swift app for **iPhone, iPad and Mac**. The layout adapts to the screen: larger devices can keep the scene, reading and explanations side by side; a phone opens explanations in a sheet.

Learning progress saves automatically on the device. There is no document picker and no learning file to manage. Cross-device progress sync is not currently offered.
