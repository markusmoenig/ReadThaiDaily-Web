The picture stays connected to the language. A lesson moves through short activities, with explanations close to the word or symbol you are exploring.

## 1. Discover

Begin with a large illustration. Tap a marker on a person, object or place to reveal the Thai word, its meaning and how it is written. Selected markers have a different highlight, so you can see what you are exploring.

<AppScreenshot name="discover" alt="Actual Discover screen on Mac" />

## 2. Talk

Meet short sentences built around the scene. Thai words are individually selectable, making word boundaries easier to see. Tap a word for its explanation; tap one of its written parts to explore that consonant or vowel.

<AppScreenshot name="talk" alt="Actual Talk screen showing a selected word and its explanation" />

**Listen** uses the device’s Thai system voice. **Slow** uses a slower speech rate for a closer listen. Available voices depend on the device; a preferred male or female voice may not be installed, so the app can use a fallback voice.

Choose **Dao** or **Din** in the toolbar to explore the supported speaker perspectives and their appropriate pronouns or polite particles. Narrated lines can refer to either character.

## 3. Build

Tap or drag consonant and vowel tiles into boxes in written order. Clicking a suggested tile also opens its illustrated explanation. This connects the shapes you see with the word you are building.

For example, น and า make **นา**, “rice field”. The same vowel with ก gives **กา**, “crow”.

## 4. Read

Try reading the familiar material yourself, then check the meaning. In the independent reading activity, picture support is hidden until you reveal a hint. A comprehension check and the use of hints are recorded separately from your reading attempt.

The chapter ends with a longer reading and comprehension questions, using vocabulary introduced in the lessons. Lesson completion unlocks that chapter reading.

## 5. Type — optional

The word remains visible. Your task is to find its consonants and vowels on a Thai keyboard, rather than recall the spelling from memory. This is optional, so you can continue learning before setting up a Thai keyboard. Welcome has no Type exercise.

## Your reference tools

The **Words** section keeps a vocabulary index and links words to the lessons where they were introduced. The **Script map** groups consonants and vowels, with the same pictures used in Learn Thai Script. You can also copy a Thai word from its explanation panel to look it up outside the app.
